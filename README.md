## 🌟 About the Project

**Re360 Project** transforms your Windows PC into an authentic, premium Xbox 360 entertainment hub. Combining the iconic **Metro Dashboard**, the legendary **3D Cover Flow Carousel**, and seamless multi-platform game synchronization, Re360 turns your library into a fluid, couch-friendly console experience.

Whether using an Xbox controller from your sofa or a keyboard at your desk, Re360 delivers the golden age of console design directly to modern PC gaming.

### Key Highlights

- 🎮 **Authentic Xbox 360 Metro Interface**: Fluid blade navigation, animated tiles, and classic console sound design.
- 💿 **3D Cover Flow Carousel**: Dynamic green Xbox 360 media boxes, 3D spine depth, tilt effects, and an interactive reactive aurora floor.
- 🏆 **Gamerscore & Achievements System**: Steam achievements automatically converted into classic Xbox 360 **G** Gamerscore (1,000 G per title).
- 🔗 **Unified Game Hub**: Bring Steam, GOG, PC executables, and emulators (Xenia & PCSX2) into one single, cohesive catalog.
- 🛡️ **Xbox Guide Menu**: Access the full Guide overlay anytime with the Xbox button or Start, with Windows Game Bar suppression for zero distractions.
- 🖼️ **High-Definition Offline Art**: High-resolution 600×900 vertical box art, panoramic hero banners, and screenshots saved directly onto your machine.

## 🚀 Installation & Getting Started

Re360 is built as a plug-and-play Windows application. **No terminal commands, compilers, or complex setups are required.**

### 1. Download & Extract
1. Download the latest release package (`Re360 Project.zip` or clone the repository).
2. Extract the folder anywhere on your PC (e.g., `C:\Games\Re360 Project` or your user folder).

### 2. Launch the Application
- Open the application folder and double-click:
  ```text
  Re360 Project.exe
  ```
- *Tip: Right-click `Re360 Project.exe` and select **Pin to Start** or **Create desktop shortcut** for one-click access.*

### 3. First-Time Setup Wizard
On your very first run, Re360 greets you with the classic setup screen:
1. **Choose your Gamertag**: Type your player name using the controller on-screen keyboard or physical keyboard.
2. **Select your Avatar**: Pick an authentic classic Xbox 360 Gamerpic or upload any custom picture from your PC.
3. **Connect Your Libraries**:
   - **Steam**: Paste your Steam profile link or SteamID to automatically sync your games, playtime, and achievements.
   - **Local PC Games & Emulators**: Click **"Autodetect"** to automatically discover installed games, Xenia (Xbox 360), and PCSX2 (PS2) without manual path hunting.
4. Click **Complete** and enter the Dashboard!

## 🗺️ System Tour — How It Works

Re360 is structured into dedicated, purpose-built interfaces designed for fast navigation:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                      RE360 DASHBOARD                            │
├─────────┬────────┬────────┬───────┬───────┬───────┬──────┬──────┤
│  bing   │  home  │ social │ games │ video │ music │ apps │ set. │
└─────────┴────────┴────────┴───────┴───────┴───────┴──────┴──────┘
    │         │        │        │       │       │       │     │
    ▼         ▼        ▼        ▼       ▼       ▼       ▼     ▼
 Quick     Hero     Gamerpic  Library Built-in Audio   Web   Theme &
 Search   Launch     Profile  & 3D    Player   Player  Apps  Deadzones
```

### 1. The Metro Dashboard
The primary landing blade organized into horizontal tabs (navigate using **LB / RB** on your controller or Left/Right arrows):

| Section | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`bing`** | Quick global search for any game in your library, featuring an Xbox on-screen keyboard. |
| **`home`** | Your primary command center: quick-launch the last played title, browse your top 3 featured games, jump to pinned favorites, or launch YouTube. |
| **`social`** | Displays your Gamertag, Gamerpic, total **G** Gamerscore, and quick links to Steam Community and friends. |
| **`games`** | Full library access: launch the 3D Collection, view installed games, browse pins, or explore the Game Store catalog. |
| **`tv & video`** | Built-in media hub automatically indexing videos from your Windows *Videos* folder with controller-friendly playback. |
| **`music`** | Background audio player scanning your Windows *Music* library. |
| **`apps`** | Direct shortcuts to web apps, media services, and Windows tools. |
| **`settings`** | Full console settings: audio volume, joystick deadzones, button remapping, themes, language, and platform sync. |

---

### 2. 3D Cover Flow Carousel (Collection Mode)
Pressing **Y** or selecting **"My Games"** opens the immersive **3D Cover Flow stage**:
- **Xbox 360 Media Cases**: Every title is enclosed in an authentic green media case with platform branding banner, glossy cellophane reflection, and 3D spine depth.
- **Reactive Aurora Borealis**: The background dynamically ripples with glowing aurora waves that respond to your movement.
- **Instant Filtering**: Filter seamlessly between **All Games**, **Installed Only**, or **Favorites** with one button.
- **Platform Switching**: Seamlessly toggle between Steam, GOG, PC Standalone, Xenia, and PCSX2.

---

### 3. Xbox Marketplace & Game Details Hub
Pressing **X** on any highlighted game opens the full store-style detail screen:
- **Panoramic Backdrop**: Fluid high-resolution artwork and wallpapers.
- **Game Facts**: Developer, publisher, release date, and tags.
- **Photo Gallery**: Screenshots and artwork retrieved and cached for offline enjoyment.
- **Custom Box Art**: Want your own custom artwork? Click **Change Cover** to apply any PNG, JPG, or WebP image.

---

### 4. Converted Gamerscore & Achievements
Re360 brings the excitement of the "Achievement Unlocked" chime back to PC:
- Steam achievements are automatically mapped to a standardized **1,000 G** per completed game.
- Track both locked and unlocked achievements with official badges, unlock timestamps, and descriptions.
- Your total Gamerscore updates across your profile header and gamer card in real-time.

---

### 5. The Xbox Guide Menu
Press the **Xbox Guide button** (or **Start** / **Home** on keyboard) at any point to summon the floating Xbox Guide:
- Powered by `NativeGuide.exe`, it intercepts controller input cleanly without triggering Windows Game Bar.
- Quick-navigate between Home, Games, Social, Controller Settings, or return directly to your session.

## 🎮 Controls & Navigation

Re360 is built from the ground up for standard Xbox / XInput controllers, while supporting full keyboard navigation:

### Controller Scheme

| Button | Action |
| :---: | :--- |
| **D-Pad / Left Stick** | Navigate tiles and game cases |
| <kbd>A</kbd> | **Select / Launch Game** |
| <kbd>B</kbd> | **Back / Return** |
| <kbd>X</kbd> | **Game Details / Options / Toggle Favorite** |
| <kbd>Y</kbd> | **Switch to 3D Library / Search** |
| <kbd>LB</kbd> / <kbd>RB</kbd> | **Switch Dashboard Tabs (Left / Right)** |
| <kbd>LT</kbd> / <kbd>RT</kbd> | **Page Scroll / Fast Carousel Seek** |
| **Xbox Guide / Start** | **Open Xbox Guide Overlay** |

### Keyboard Shortcuts

| Key | Action |
| :---: | :--- |
| <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> | Navigate interface |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> / <kbd>Space</kbd> | Select / Play |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> / <kbd>Backspace</kbd> | Back |
| <kbd>X</kbd> | Options / Favorite |
| <kbd>Y</kbd> | Search / Library |
| <kbd>F</kbd> | Toggle Fullscreen |
| <kbd>Home</kbd> | Open Guide Menu |

## 🕹️ Supported Platforms

Re360 serves as a single front-end for your entire game collection:

| Platform | Features |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Steam** | Automatic library sync, playtime tracking, achievements converted to Gamerscore, native one-click execution. |
| **GOG** | Seamless login authorization, automatic metadata fetching, and direct game launch. |
| **Xenia (Xbox 360 Emulator)** | Scans and runs `.iso`, `.xex`, `.zar`, and STFS/XBLA arcade packages directly with fullscreen support. |
| **PCSX2 (PlayStation 2 Emulator)** | Automatically indexes `.iso`, `.bin`, `.chd`, `.cso`, `.gz`, and `.elf` files with clean boot parameters. |
| **PC Executables (.exe)** | Add any standalone PC game or launcher with custom artwork and instant execution. |

## 🎨 Customization

Tailor your console experience to match your exact aesthetic:
- **Gamerpics**: Choose from iconic 2000s Xbox 360 profile pictures or upload your own avatar.
- **Themes & Accents**: Select from classic dark styles, high-contrast themes, or glowing neon aurora waves.
- **Sound Options**: Toggle classic Xbox UI sound effects on or off and adjust volume levels.
- **Controller Calibration**: Adjust stick deadzones from 5% to 40% to eliminate stick drift on older controllers.
- **Multi-Language Support**: Fully localized in **English**, **Português (Brasil)**, and **Español**.

---

## 🔒 Privacy & Offline First

- **100% Local Storage**: Your credentials, keys, profiles, and media records reside exclusively on your machine in the `.local/` folder.
- **No Telemetry or Tracking**: No analytical tracking, ads, or third-party user data collection.
- **Offline Capable**: All covers, logos, and metadata are automatically cached into `assets/offline-art/`. Once synced, your entire library renders instantly even without an active internet connection.
